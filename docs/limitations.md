# Limits of interpretation
- Released checkpoints differ in training, size, precision and prompt interface.
- Geometric sensitivity does not establish represented graph content or causal use.
- A node-embedding readout does not prove BFS.
- Bootstrap intervals over graphs do not measure training-seed uncertainty.
- Pointwise intervals crossing zero do not establish equivalence.
- The common-backbone pilot covers one simple topology and one training seed.
- DIRECT matched EXPAND on development accuracy at lower measured cost.
- Final question access is a possible workspace bypass, not an established cause.
- Fixed hybrids and an RQ3 improvement were not evaluated in the supplied pilot.
